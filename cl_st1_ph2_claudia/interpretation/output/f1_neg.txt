## Factor f1 (negative pole): **Affectionate affirmation and images of well-being**

This pole is best interpreted as a discourse of **valuing love, life, and belonging through evaluative language and imagery of sweetness, light, and natural beauty**. It includes romantic and sensual affection, appreciation of the world, and narratives of renewed hope. These meanings are not uniformly cheerful: some texts invoke sunshine or sweetness within accounts of deprivation, disappointment, or contested belonging.

### Possible labels

1. **Affectionate affirmation and images of well-being** — the strongest overall label. It accommodates positive evaluation, romantic pleasure, environmental imagery, and desired or recovered happiness without assuming that every text expresses uncomplicated optimism.
2. **Sweetness, sunshine, and loving attachment** — a more lexically transparent label, foregrounding the pole’s principal semantic clusters.
3. **Idealised love, life, and belonging** — captures the discourse functions illustrated by the excerpts, although “idealised” is less appropriate for the prison narrative and should not be treated as universal.

### Lexical evidence

The seven loadings support three overlapping clusters:

| Cluster | Loading words | Interpretive contribution |
|---|---|---|
| Positive evaluation and pleasure | **wonderful** (−1.17), **sweet** (−.39) | Appreciation, desirability, pleasurable experience, and affectionate valuation |
| Loving and interpersonal orientation | **lovin** (−1.05), **gentleman** (−.58) | Affection and interpersonal reference; *gentleman* is less securely interpretable without contextual examples |
| Light, atmosphere, and animating feeling | **sunshine** (−.85), **cloud** (−.78), **spirit** (−.46) | Natural imagery associated with well-being, emotional conditions, aspiration, or felt vitality |

The strongest loading, **wonderful**, supports affirmative evaluation, while **lovin** gives substantial weight to affectionate or sensual attachment. **Sunshine** and **cloud** indicate that atmospheric imagery is central rather than incidental.

However, neither *cloud* nor *spirit* is inherently positive, and *sweet* can describe romantic pleasure or attachment to home. The pole should therefore be understood as a recurrent lexical configuration, not simply as a measure of positive sentiment. The excerpts help establish how these words function in discourse.

### Evidence from the excerpts

**Appreciation of life and human connection.**  
In t000859, repeated **wonderful** evaluates the world through trees, flowers, skies, **clouds**, friendship, and declarations of love. Here, natural beauty and everyday social connection jointly construct an affirming view of existence.

**Recovery, hope, and emotional transformation.**  
In t000838, “dark **clouds**” represent impediments and distress that have disappeared, allowing renewed confidence. In t000821, **sunshine** is initially something needed but not received; the larger narrative moves from disappointment toward belief in love. These examples support a discourse of **desired or regained well-being**, rather than merely static happiness.

T000864 similarly uses flight “above the **clouds**” alongside smiling, rainbows, and an admired person, connecting atmospheric imagery with buoyancy and idealised personal qualities.

**Romantic sweetness and sensual affirmation.**  
T000835 combines **sweet** kisses with “summer **sunshine**,” presenting intimacy as pleasurable warmth. T000901 develops **sweet** through edible metaphors, affectionate repetition, and bodily enjoyment. T000938 explicitly joins **sweet** and **wonderful** in an affirmation of life and sexual intimacy. Its visible references to *lovin’* and *spirit* also connect affection with bodily feeling and spiritualised permission.

**Belonging and unavailable well-being.**  
In t000982, **sweet** evaluates home and regional attachment. Yet the surrounding political references mean that this is not simply a universally harmonious celebration. In t000937, the speaker has not seen **sunshine** because of imprisonment: light signifies a valued condition that is unavailable. This is an important limit on an exclusively optimistic interpretation.

There are also discrepancies between the supplied keyword annotations and the visible excerpts. **Sunshine** is listed for t000896 but does not appear in the displayed passage; its harmony and liberation discourse is therefore contextual evidence, not a visible instance of that loading word. Conversely, *lovin’* and *spirit* occur in some passages without being included in their keyword summaries. **Gentleman** is not illustrated, so its contribution remains underdetermined.

### Decade pattern

For this negative pole, the relevant ordering is from the **lowest** mean factor score upward:

| Decade stratum | Mean fac1 |
|---|---:|
| **2020** | **1.836** |
| **2010** | **2.130** |
| **2000** | **3.227** |

The **2020 stratum is most oriented toward this pole**, followed by 2010 and then 2000. All supplied examples come from 2020, consistent with that ordering, although they cannot establish how the discourse is realised in the earlier strata.

The means decline across the three decades, suggesting a progressively stronger **relative orientation toward this negative-pole lexical pattern** in the curated transcript sample. All means nevertheless remain positive; this is not evidence that the negative pole predominates absolutely. The sizeable standard deviations also counsel against treating decade differences as categorical or statistically established.

Finally, the familiar older repertoire in several 2020 examples should not be reassigned to earlier decades. The supplied strata are authoritative: this pattern concerns the project’s selected transcripts, not a demonstrated historical emergence of these discourses in songwriting.

**Recommended label: “Affectionate affirmation and images of well-being.”** It captures the shared lexical pattern while retaining room for well-being to be celebrated, recovered, desired, or denied.
