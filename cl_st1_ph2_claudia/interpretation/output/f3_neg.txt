## Factor f3 (negative): **Embodied desire and intimate engagement**

The most defensible interpretation of this pole is a discourse of **bodily attraction, sensual contact, and emotionally charged engagement with a desired partner**. It includes flirtation and sexual invitation, but also romantic attachment and longing for lost intimacy. A secondary strand involves sexually charged self-presentation and club-oriented performance.

### Possible labels

| Proposed label | Justification |
|---|---|
| **Embodied desire and intimate engagement** | **Preferred.** Captures the bodily vocabulary and accommodates attraction, sexual invitation, romantic attachment, and separation-related longing. |
| **Sensual attraction and romantic longing** | Makes the principal discourse patterns in the examples explicit, particularly the connection between physical contact and emotional need. |
| **Flirtation, bodily intimacy, and sexual self-presentation** | Highlights courtship, erotic invitation, and the club-oriented or boastful strand, although it is less economical and gives less prominence to attachment. |

### 1. Lexical evidence

The clearest semantic cluster consists of **lip** (−.75), **touch** (−.57), and **stare** (−.50). These words locate interpersonal engagement in the body: lips evoke kissing and erotic attention, touch evokes physical proximity, and staring foregrounds sustained attention to another person. Together, they support an interpretation centred on **embodied attraction and intimacy**, rather than romantic feeling alone.

The strongest loading, **mmm** (−.92), adds a vocal-interjectional component. In the examples, it accompanies romantic address, flirtation, and sexualised performance. However, it also occurs in a rap passage concerned with lifestyle and self-presentation. It is therefore better interpreted as a marker of **affective or performative vocal expression** whose sensual significance depends on context, not as an inherently erotic word.

**Handle** (−.68) is also contextually variable. In excerpt 008, “I had to handle that” participates in a sexualised account of attraction. In excerpt 003, “the ROC handle like Van Exel” belongs to a boastful rap passage rather than intimate contact. Its substantial loading contributes to the pole, but does not independently establish a coherent intimacy meaning.

The remaining loadings provide less secure thematic evidence:

- **Chick** (−.49) is compatible with colloquial reference to women and potentially with attraction or sexualised social interaction, but is not illustrated in the supplied excerpts.
- **Question** (−.36) occurs in courtship hesitation and in the formulation “It asks no questions why”; it does not make this an information-seeking discourse.
- **Cloud** and **sunshine** (both −.36) are not illustrated here. Their role cannot confidently be specified from these samples.

Thus, the interpretation rests on a clear bodily-interpersonal cluster while recognising that not every loading has a uniformly sensual meaning.

### 2. Evidence from the high-scoring texts

The examples show several related manifestations of this discourse:

- **Longing for physical and emotional closeness:** Excerpt 001 combines **mmm, lip, touch** with regret over separation—“your touch” and “kiss your lips” represent the intimacy the speaker has lost.
- **Erotic appraisal and flirtation:** Excerpt 002 combines **mmm, lip, stare** with looking at an attractive man, bodily appraisal, and explicit sexual invitation.
- **Intense romantic attachment:** Excerpt 003 links **stare** and **touch** to fascination, repeated contact, and emotional loss of composure.
- **Courtship and sexual invitation:** Excerpt 004 uses **lip, touch, question** in a narrative of nervous approach and an invitation to physical intimacy. Excerpt 005 associates **mmm** and **lip** with attraction, bodily arousal, and conditions placed on intimacy.
- **Sensual pleasure and sexualised performance:** Excerpt 010 connects **touch** with heat, desire, and sensation; excerpt 008 embeds **mmm** and **handle** in club-based bodily display and sexual boasting.

The fit is not uniform. Excerpts 006 and 009 foreground assertion or lifestyle performance, while excerpt 007 places lips within grotesque sexualised humour. These cases indicate that the pole is **not exclusively tender or romantic**.

There is also an evidence limitation: **touch** is listed for excerpts 006–007, and **question** for excerpt 009, but these words are not visible in the supplied passages. Their reported presence should not be given an invented local interpretation.

### 3. Decade pattern

The **2000 stratum drives the negative pole**:

| Decade | Mean fac3 |
|---|---:|
| 2000 | −0.230 |
| 2010 | +0.038 |
| 2020 | +0.164 |

All ten supplied examples belong to the 2000 stratum, reinforcing its association with this discourse. The progressively higher means suggest **declining average alignment with the negative-pole lexical pattern** across the included decades.

This is a descriptive tendency, not evidence that sensual or intimate discourse disappears. The standard deviations of approximately 0.67–0.75 indicate considerable within-decade variation, and no significance tests are supplied. The decade assignments should also be treated as corpus strata, not independently reconstructed from song release dates.

**Recommended label: _Embodied desire and intimate engagement_.** It captures the convergence between the loadings and examples without reducing the pole to explicit sexuality or extending the interpretation beyond the verbal transcript evidence.
