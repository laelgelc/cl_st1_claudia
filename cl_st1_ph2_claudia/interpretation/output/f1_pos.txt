## Factor f1 (positive pole): **Affectionate appreciation and celebratory sociability**

This pole is best interpreted as a discourse of **valuing love, life, people, and belonging through affectionate, appreciative, and celebratory language**. Its coherence is primarily evaluative and interpersonal rather than narrowly topical: romantic intimacy, appreciation of the world, festive togetherness, attachment to home, and courteous audience address all contribute.

This interpretation concerns the **verbal transcripts** in the curated sample. It does not establish corresponding patterns in the videos’ music, sound, or imagery.

### Possible labels

| Label | Justification |
|---|---|
| **Affectionate appreciation and celebratory sociability** | Recommended. Captures both the strong loving/evaluative vocabulary and the public, communal uses of *spirit* and *gentleman*. |
| **Warmth, love, and affirmative belonging** | Emphasises romantic affection, appreciation of life, and attachment to people or home. “Affirmative” describes the main discourse tendency, not every excerpt’s overall tone. |
| **Sentimental affirmation and convivial address** | Highlights sweetness, emotional uplift, festive participation, and performer–audience courtesies. Somewhat more interpretive because sentimentality is not equally evident throughout. |

A label restricted to **romantic love**, **optimism**, or **nostalgia** would be too narrow.

### Lexical basis

The strongest loadings, **wonderful (1.23)** and **lovin (1.21)**, establish the clearest semantic centre: favourable evaluation and affectionate engagement. **Sweet (.32)** extends this centre through pleasurable sensory evaluation, endearment, and affectionate attachment, although its lower loading makes it less defining statistically.

The remaining words broaden the discourse:

- **Cloud (.81)** and **sunshine (.67)** provide an environmental vocabulary that can express beauty, emotional conditions, or imagined relief.
- **Spirit (.71)** connects intimate feeling with festive or spiritually inflected participation.
- **Gentleman (.65)** introduces courteous social address, especially in the formula “ladies and gentlemen.”

The full loading set therefore supports **warm evaluative and relational discourse**, but not an exclusively cheerful semantic field. *Cloud* and *sunshine*, in particular, acquire their significance from context.

### How the excerpts realise the pole

**Affectionate and sensual affirmation** is most clearly represented in excerpt 001, which combines four loading words: *wonderful, lovin, spirit,* and *sweet*. “There’s nothing wrong with me lovin’ you” legitimises intimacy, while “how sweet and wonderful life can be” links loving contact to a positive evaluation of life. “If the spirit moves you” adds spiritually inflected language to bodily desire. This is not simply abstract positivity: it is an invitation to intimate participation.

Excerpts 007 and 010 develop a related discourse through sweetness metaphors. In excerpt 010, **sweet** describes a kiss and the promised quality of the addressee’s life, while **sunshine** supplies a pleasurable comparison. Excerpt 007 likewise frames love as appetising and satisfying through “sweet thing.” These examples support the affectionate component without making all uses of *sweet* romantic.

**Appreciative world-description and emotional uplift** appear in excerpts 003 and 009. In excerpt 003, **wonderful** evaluates the world, while **cloud** occurs within a scene of natural beauty and social goodwill. In excerpt 009, the departure of “dark clouds” represents recovery from pain and obstructed perception. The positive orientation comes from the clouds’ disappearance, not from *cloud* being inherently positive.

**Festive belonging and courteous public interaction** form another substantial strand. Excerpt 005 uses **spirit** in “Christmas spirit,” supporting communal celebration and sentimental participation. Excerpts 002 and 004 combine **wonderful** with the address formula represented by **gentleman**. Here the pole is partly realised through spoken introductions, thanks, audience recognition, and blessings. Especially in excerpt 004, these loading words occur around song lyrics that are not themselves affectionate or uplifting. The factor therefore captures performance-related social language as well as sung content.

Two examples qualify the interpretation:

- Excerpt 008 uses **sweet** to express attachment to home, alongside contentious regional and political references. Positive attachment does not make the whole text unambiguously convivial.
- Excerpt 006 contains **sunshine** as something the imprisoned speaker has not seen. This is deprivation and longing, not straightforward celebration.

These qualifications favour a label centred on **affectionate appreciation and social warmth**, rather than uniform happiness.

### Decade pattern

The authoritative decade means are:

| Decade | Mean f1 |
|---|---:|
| 2000s | −2.952 |
| 2010s | −1.992 |
| 2020s | −1.630 |

The **2020s score most strongly toward this positive pole**, followed by the 2010s and then the 2000s. The means suggest a progressive shift toward the pole across the sampled strata. However, **all three means remain negative**: the trend indicates increasing relative alignment, not positive-pole dominance.

All supplied high-scoring excerpts are assigned to the 2020s, reinforcing that stratum’s importance in the illustrative evidence. Nevertheless, their familiar older repertoire should not be treated as evidence that this discourse originated in the 2020s. The pattern concerns the project’s **decade-assigned transcript sample**, not necessarily the dates of song composition or original performance.

Overall, **Affectionate appreciation and celebratory sociability** offers the most inclusive label while preserving the pole’s contextual variation.
